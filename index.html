What Is La Casita?

🏠

The Pop-Up

La Casita is a Puerto Rican–inspired reggaeton experience in Brooklyn, built to capture the feeling of being in Puerto Rico for the night. Real music, real energy, and a space that feels like an island house party right in NYC.

🎵

The Sound

Non-stop perreo. Old school reggaeton, dembow, Bad Bunny, and all the Latin hits that make you move. Top DJs all night.

🍴

The Vibes

Tropical drinks, Latin street food, neon lights, and an energy you won't find anywhere else in Brooklyn.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is La Casita BK?

La Casita BK is a Puerto Rican-inspired reggaeton pop-up experience in Brooklyn, NYC. Live DJ sets, tropical cocktails, Latin street food, neon lights, immersive photo ops, and an island house party vibe all night long. Presented by Pulse Hospitality Group.

When does La Casita BK open?

La Casita BK opens May 1st & 2nd, 2026 in Brooklyn, NYC. Presale tickets go live Friday April 3, 2026 at 7PM EST. Sign up above to get early access to tickets and VIP tables.

What kind of music is played?

Non-stop reggaeton, perreo, dembow, and Latin hits. Old school reggaeton classics, Bad Bunny, and the latest Latin music spun by top DJs all night.

Where is it located?

La Casita BK is in Brooklyn, NYC. The exact venue address will be announced closer to the opening date. Follow @lacasita.bk on Instagram for updates.
