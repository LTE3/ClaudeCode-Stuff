A night you won’t forget

Four things you get every single night. That’s the whole promise — no invented attractions, no scare actors.

The main floor

Reggaetón, perreo, dembow and Latin club all night. No filler sets, no genre detours — the sound the room is known for.

Fog and candlelight

Low light, heavy fog and decor that gets more unhinged every week. Atmosphere, not jump scares — nobody is going to grab you.

Costume weekend

Costumes welcome all season, expected once October starts. Halloween weekend is the peak: October 30 and 31, back to back.

Tables & bottles

Reserved high-tops and couches with bottle service. Skip the line, keep your spot, watch the whole room from a seat.

Before you come

  • 21 and over, no exceptions. Physical government photo ID — not a picture on your phone.
  • Fog machines and low light throughout. No strobe-heavy effects on standard nights.
  • Costumes yes, full-face masks no — security has to be able to match you to your ID.
  • Nothing that reads as a real weapon, including props. It will be turned away at the door.
  • Free-before-midnight is capped every night. When the list fills, it closes.
  • Management reserves the right to refuse entry. Be decent to staff and to each other.
