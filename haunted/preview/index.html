The Haunted Mansion

Ten complete websites, built from the two sites you picked and nothing else. Every one is fully clickable — Home, Dates, Tickets & tables, Plan your visit. Same real dates and same real prices across all ten.

Dominion of Terror and Nightmare in Nassau are the only source material. Each one is built once, then dressed five ways. Inside a group the sites are structurally identical — same page order, same modules, same HTML, down to the tag. Only the palette, the type and the texture change, so what you are choosing between is the look, not the layout. Pick the structure first, then the skin.

What's real and what isn't
  • Real: all 26 nights (Fri + Sat, Sept 4 – Nov 28), the address, 21+, and the Halloween weekend highlight. The email/phone signup form on every one of them genuinely writes to Supabase.
  • Real prices: $10 / $15 / $20 advance GA and $50 tables are exactly what the room ran all summer 2026. Halloween weekend ($25 / $35 / $45, tables $75) is my proposal, not a decision — it's one commented block to change, and every direction carries the same numbers.
  • The "attractions" are the room, not rooms. Music, decor and fog, costume weekend, table service — programming 428 Johnson already controls. No maze, no actors, no walkthrough is invented anywhere.
  • Deliberately off: every ticket button is disabled. Nothing here can charge a card — no events are activated and no live checkout runs from a preview.
  • Test signups are tagged Preview / Test in the database so they don't pollute the real list, and get swapped to the live tag when a direction is promoted.
  • Social links in the footers are placeholders — I don't have the real handles and won't guess. One line to fill in per site.
  • These pages are noindex — they won't show up in Google, but anyone with the link can open them.
