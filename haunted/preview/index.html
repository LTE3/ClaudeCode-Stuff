What's real and what isn't
  • Real: all 26 nights (Fri + Sat, Sept 4 – Nov 28), the address, 21+, and the Halloween weekend highlight. The email/phone signup form on every one of the twenty genuinely writes to Supabase.
  • Real prices: $10 / $15 / $20 advance GA and $50 tables are exactly what the room ran all summer 2026. Halloween weekend ($25 / $35 / $45, tables $75) is my proposal, not a decision — it's one commented block to change. Directions 1 and 2 still show the old $20 placeholder; everything else carries the real ladder.
  • The "attractions" on the round-two pages are the room, not rooms. Music, decor and fog, costume weekend, table service — programming 428 Johnson already controls. No maze, no actors, no walkthrough is invented anywhere.
  • Deliberately off: every ticket button is disabled. Nothing here can charge a card — no events are activated and no live checkout runs from a preview.
  • Test signups are tagged Preview / Test in the database so they don't pollute the real list, and get swapped to the live tag when a direction is promoted.
  • Social links in the round-two footers are placeholders — I don't have the real handles and won't guess. One line to fill in per site.
  • These pages are noindex — they won't show up in Google, but anyone with the link can open them.
