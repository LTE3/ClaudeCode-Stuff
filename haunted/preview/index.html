What's real and what isn't
  • Real: all 26 nights (Fri + Sat, Sept 4 – Nov 28), the address, 21+, and the Halloween weekend highlight. The email/phone signup form on every one of the ten genuinely writes to Supabase.
  • Real prices: $10 / $15 / $20 advance GA and $50 tables are exactly what the room ran all summer 2026. Halloween weekend ($25 / $35 / $45, tables $75) is my proposal, not a decision — it's one commented block to change. Directions 1 and 2 still show the old $20 placeholder; directions 3–9 and the recommended build all carry the real ladder.
  • Deliberately off: every ticket button is disabled. Nothing here can charge a card — no events are activated and no live checkout runs from a preview.
  • Test signups are tagged Preview / Test in the database so they don't pollute the real list, and get swapped to the live tag when a direction is promoted.
  • These pages are noindex — they won't show up in Google, but anyone with the link can open them.
