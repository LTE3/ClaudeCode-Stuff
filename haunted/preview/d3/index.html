The main floor

Reggaetón, perreo, dembow and Latin club, all night, every night. No filler sets and no genre detours — the sound the room is already known for, in a room that has been fogged out and lit by candle.

About the room

One room, run hard

428 Johnson Ave is a Brooklyn reggaetón room. For the 2026 Halloween season it runs as The Haunted Mansion: same floor, same sound, dressed for the season and pointed at one thing for twenty-six nights.

Every Friday and Saturday from September 4 to November 28. Nothing gets added mid-season and nothing gets quietly dropped.

Groups & buyouts

Ten people or a hundred. Reserved high-tops and couches with bottle service, a host at the door, and your whole group in one place instead of scattered across the room.

  • Birthdays and group tables
  • Full buyouts on selected nights
  • Press and partnership enquiries

Email the night and your headcount and you get real options back: leavetheearthllc@gmail.com

House rules

  • 21 and over, no exceptions. Physical government photo ID — not a picture on your phone.
  • Fog machines and low light throughout. No strobe-heavy effects on standard nights.
  • Costumes yes, full-face masks no — security has to be able to match you to your ID.
  • Nothing that reads as a real weapon, including props. It will be turned away at the door.
  • Free-before-midnight is capped every night. When the list fills, it closes.
  • Management reserves the right to refuse entry. Be decent to staff and to each other.
