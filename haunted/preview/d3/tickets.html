How it works

The free list

RSVP with your name and phone. Arrive before 12:00am, give the name at the door and walk in — nothing to pay, nothing to print. The list is capped every night and closes when it fills. The ladies free list runs alongside it on the same terms.

Tables

High-tops from $50, couches from $100, limited every night. A deposit holds it and applies to your tab. Your host meets you at the door.

Halloween weekend

October 30 and 31 are priced separately: $25 advance rising to $45, tables from $75, and a tighter cap on the free list. Those two nights sell first.

Two price sets

Standard nights, and Halloween weekend. That is the whole pricing story.
