Closing night

Nights worth planning around

Friday, Sept 4

Opening night. The decor is brand new and the room hasn’t been seen like this before. Expect the biggest door of the first month.

Friday, Oct 30

Halloween eve. Costumes fully expected. Peak pricing, tighter free-list cap, tables go first.

Saturday, Oct 31

Halloween itself — on a Saturday this year. The single biggest night of the season. Book anything you want in advance.

Saturday, Nov 28

Closing night, the Saturday after Thanksgiving. Everybody’s home for the weekend and the season ends here.

One more thing

Nothing is added mid-season

Twenty-six nights is the whole run — no surprise Thursdays, no pop-up dates. If it isn’t on this page, we’re closed.
