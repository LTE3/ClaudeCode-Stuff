How the free list works

RSVP with your name and phone. Arrive before 12:00am, give the name at the door, walk in — nothing to pay, nothing to print. The list is capped every night and closes when it fills, so earlier in the week is safer than Friday afternoon.

The ladies free list runs alongside it on the same terms.

How tables work

High-tops start at $50, couches at $100, and there are a limited number every night. You put down a deposit to hold it and the deposit applies to your tab. Your host meets you at the door — no line, no hunting for your group.

Halloween weekend tables start at $75 and go first. Email leavetheearthllc@gmail.com with the night and your headcount.

Fine print, plainly

  • 21 and over with physical government photo ID. No ID, no entry, no refund.
  • Advance tickets are non-refundable but transferable — send the confirmation to whoever’s going instead.
  • Free-before-midnight is capped nightly. An RSVP is a spot on a list, not a guarantee once it closes.
  • Advance price is all-in. What you see is what you pay.
  • Table deposits apply to your tab and are non-refundable inside 48 hours of the night.
  • Prices on this page are provisional pending final sign-off.
