Before you come

House rules.

  • 21 and over, no exceptions. Physical government photo ID — not a picture on your phone.
  • Fog machines and low light throughout. No strobe-heavy effects on standard nights.
  • Costumes yes, full-face masks no — security has to be able to match you to your ID.
  • Nothing that reads as a real weapon, including props. It will be turned away at the door.
  • Free-before-midnight is capped every night. When the list fills it closes.
  • Management reserves the right to refuse entry. Be decent to staff and to each other.
Questions

Do I really get in free?

Yes — RSVP and arrive before midnight and there is nothing to pay at the door. The free list is capped each night, so it closes when it fills.

How old do I have to be?

21 and over, no exceptions. Bring a valid government-issued photo ID — physical, not a photo on your phone.

Is there a dress code?

Come dressed for a party, not a hike. Costumes are welcome all season and strongly encouraged in October — no full-face masks at the door, and nothing that looks like a real weapon.

What's the music?

Reggaetón, perreo, dembow and Latin club, all night, every night. Same sound the room is known for.

How do tables work?

Reserved high-tops and couches with bottle service, limited each night. You pay a deposit to hold it and it applies to your tab. Your host meets you at the door.

Is it actually scary?

It's a party in a haunted house, not a haunted house with a party in it. Fog, candlelight, decor and a lot of atmosphere — nobody is going to jump out at you.

Can I get in after midnight?

Yes — that's what general admission is for. Buy in advance and it's cheaper than the door, and the price steps up as each night sells.

Can I book for a group?

Birthdays, group tables and buyouts — email us with the night and the headcount and we'll come back with options. leavetheearthllc@gmail.com
