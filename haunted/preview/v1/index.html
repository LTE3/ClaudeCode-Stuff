The season

Same room. Something else living in it.

Three months, one address, and a house that gets stranger every weekend. Come for the music, stay because the door locked behind you.

The music

Reggaetón, perreo, dembow and Latin club all night. The same sound the room was built for — louder, later, and considerably more haunted.

The house

The whole space dressed for the season. Candlelight, fog, and corners you probably shouldn't wander into alone.

The tables

Bottle service and reserved high-tops for groups who'd rather haunt from a good seat. Limited each night.

The peak
