Questions

How old do I have to be?

21 and over, no exceptions. Valid government-issued photo ID — physical, not a picture on your phone.

Dress code?

Dress for a party. Costumes welcome all season, strongly encouraged in October. No full-face masks at the door, nothing that looks like a real weapon.

What's the music?

Reggaetón, perreo, dembow and Latin club — all night, every night.

Can I reserve a table?

Yes. Reserved high-tops with bottle service, limited each night. Packages and deposits go live with ticket sales.

Is it actually scary?

It's a party in a haunted house, not a haunted house with a party in it. Fog, black light and atmosphere — nobody jumps out at you.
