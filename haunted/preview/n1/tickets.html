How the free list works

RSVP with your name and phone. Arrive before 12:00am, give the name at the door and walk in — nothing to pay, nothing to print. The list is capped every night and closes when it fills.

The ladies free list runs alongside it on the same terms.

How tables work

High-tops from $50, couches from $100, limited every night. A deposit holds it and applies to your tab. Your host meets you at the door.

Halloween weekend tables start at $75 and go first. Email leavetheearthllc@gmail.com with the night and headcount.

Fine print, plainly

  • 21 and over with physical government photo ID. No ID, no entry, no refund.

  • Advance price is all-in — what you see is what you pay.
  • Table deposits apply to your tab, non-refundable inside 48 hours of the night.
