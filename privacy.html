Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pulse Hospitality Group ("La Casita BK," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit lacasitabk.com, join our guest list, purchase tickets, or attend our events.

Information We Collect

  • Contact & signup info — name, phone number, and email address you provide when joining the guest list or buying tickets.
  • Purchase info — ticket type, quantity, and order details. Card payments are processed by Stripe; we do not store your full card number.
  • Usage info — basic, non-identifying analytics such as the page visited and the referring site, used to understand traffic.

How We Use It

  • To process ticket orders, manage the guest list, and admit you to events.
  • To send you event updates, reminders, and promotional messages by text and email (see Text Messaging below).
  • To operate, secure, and improve our website and events.

Text Messaging (SMS)

If you give us your phone number, you agree we may send you transactional and marketing text messages about La Casita BK events. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message; reply HELP for help. Consent to texts is not a condition of purchase. We do not sell or share your phone number with third parties for their own marketing.

Who We Share It With

We share information only with service providers who help us run the business, and only as needed:

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Supabase — secure database and hosting of signups/orders.
  • Twilio — delivery of SMS/text messages.
  • Google (Gmail) — delivery of transactional and marketing email.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of our guests and staff. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies & Analytics

We use minimal browser storage to remember your cart and to record a single anonymous page view per visit. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

Data Retention & Security

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. Data is stored with access controls so that customer contact details are not publicly readable.

Your Choices

  • Opt out of texts by replying STOP; opt out of emails using the unsubscribe link.
  • Request a copy or deletion of your personal information by contacting us below.

Age

Our events and website are intended for adults 21 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.
